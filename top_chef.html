🦆Gabes Tacos

Welcome to Gabes Tacos. We’re your neighborhood taco truck in Times Square, serving up good food and friendly service every day. Hungry? Check out our menu below.

About Gabes Tacos

At Gabes Tacos, we believe tacos make everything better. Our family started this food truck to bring something new to our New York neighbors and visitors from all over. We use fresh ingredients in everything we serve.

People enjoy our bright red truck, our creative specials, and the way we make everyone feel at home. Stop by for lunch, dinner, or just because you want something delicious.

Top Sellers

  • Taco de Pato (Duck Taco) – rich, savory, and unforgettable
  • Taco de Cerdo (Pork Taco) – sweet and spicy, always a hit
  • Taco de Camarón (Shrimp Taco) – light, bright, and full of flavor

Taco de Pato

Our famous duck taco is slow-cooked until it’s tender, then topped with sautéed onions and smoky chipotle sauce. It’s the one everyone talks about.

Taco de Pollo

Grilled chicken that’s juicy and full of flavor, with tomato, cilantro, and a sprinkle of queso fresco. Simple, classic, and tasty.

Taco de Res

This beef taco is loaded with juicy steak strips, fresh onion, green salsa, and cilantro. Comfort food with a kick.

Taco de Cerdo

Our pork al pastor is marinated in chili and pineapple, then grilled just right. Sweet, spicy, and a crowd-pleaser.

Taco de Cordero

If you’re in the mood for something different, try our lamb taco. It’s tender, herby, and comes with mild red salsa on a warm tortilla.

Taco de Pescado

Crispy fried fish, crunchy cabbage, chipotle-lime crema, and a squeeze of fresh lime. Great for a sunny day or any day.

Taco de Camarón

Shrimp sautéed in garlic and butter, topped with pico de gallo and creamy avocado slices. Bright, fresh, and satisfying.

Find Us

You can’t miss our bright red truck at Times Square, right on the corner of 7th Ave and 44th St, New York City. Come say hi.
