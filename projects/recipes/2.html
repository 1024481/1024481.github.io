Chicken Biryani

My family always looked forward to special occasions because it meant someone would make chicken biryani. My grandma always said, Biryani brings everyone to the table. Over time, we tried different versions, but this classic recipe remained everyone’s favorite. The layers of rice, spices, and chicken bring back memories of celebrations and big family meals.

Tips & Tricks

  • Soak the rice for at least 30 minutes for fluffy grains.
  • Use bone-in chicken for richer flavor.
  • Garnish with fried onions and fresh cilantro.

Recipe

IngredientAmount
Basmati rice2 cups
Chicken (bone-in pieces)1.5 lbs
Yogurt1/2 cup
Onions, thinly sliced2 large
Ginger-garlic paste2 tbsp
Tomato, chopped1 medium
Biryani masala2 tbsp
Turmeric powder1/2 tsp
Chili powder1 tsp
Whole spices (bay leaf, cinnamon, cardamom, cloves)1 tbsp total
Oil or ghee1/4 cup
Water3 cups
Saltto taste
Instructions: 1. Soak rice 30 mins. Fry onions until golden, set aside half for garnish.
2. In same pot, add whole spices, ginger-garlic paste, tomato; cook 2 mins.
3. Add chicken, yogurt, biryani masala, turmeric, chili, salt. Cook until chicken is browned.
4. Layer rice over chicken, add water. Cover and cook on low until rice is tender and chicken is cooked through.
5. Top with fried onions and cilantro. Serve hot.
Prep Time30 mins
Servings6
