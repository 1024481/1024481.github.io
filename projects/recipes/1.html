Salmon Tikka Masala

My mom wanted something different because chicken tikka masala started to feel repetitive after a while, so she decided to try making it with salmon instead. We needed something besides chicken for a change! The flavors turned out to work really well together. This recipe combines our love of Indian spices with fresh salmon for a dish that always feels special and a little unexpected.

Tips & Tricks

  • Marinate the salmon for at least 1 hour for best flavor.
  • Use Greek yogurt for a creamier sauce.
  • Substitute coconut milk for dairy-free version.

Recipe

Ingredient Amount
Salmon fillets1 lb (about 2 fillets)
Plain yogurt1/2 cup
Lemon juice2 tbsp
Garam masala2 tsp
Ground cumin1 tsp
Ground coriander1 tsp
Salt1/2 tsp
Olive oil2 tbsp
Onion, chopped1 medium
Garlic, minced3 cloves
Ginger, grated1 tbsp
Tomato puree1 cup
Heavy cream or coconut milk1/2 cup
Instructions: 1. Mix yogurt, lemon juice, garam masala, cumin, coriander, and salt. Marinate salmon for 1 hour.
2. Grill or pan-sear salmon until just cooked. Set aside.
3. In a pan, heat oil. Sauté onion, garlic, and ginger until golden.
4. Add tomato puree, cook 5 mins. Stir in cream (or coconut milk), simmer 2 mins.
5. Add salmon to sauce, simmer 5 mins. Serve with rice.
Prep Time20 mins (plus 1 hr marinate)
Servings4
